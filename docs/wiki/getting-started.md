# Getting started

[Wiki home](README.md) · [Notebooks](notebooks.md) · [Writing](writing.md) · [Backups](data-and-settings.md)

Folio runs on Android 8.0 and newer, and core notebooks work completely offline. Layouts adapt to screen width, so navigation may appear as a bottom bar, a rail, or a sidebar.

## Create or import a notebook

1. Open **Library** and tap **Create or import**.
2. Choose **New notebook**, **Quick note**, or **Infinite canvas** (you can also create a folder here).
3. To annotate a PDF, choose **Import PDF document** and pick the file. To open a portable notebook, choose **Import Folio backup** and pick its `.folio` file.
4. Open the notebook from the Library, pick a tool in the toolbar, and start writing, or switch to the hand tool to pan.

Importing a PDF copies it into Folio; edits never modify the original file on your device. See [Notebooks and the library](notebooks.md) for pages, folders, search, and Trash.

## Make your first edit

- **Write and highlight:** Pick the pen or highlighter and write on the page. Tap the active tool again to adjust its width, colour, or brush.
- **Erase and select:** Use the eraser to remove ink, or the lasso to move, resize, restyle, or tidy content. Undo and redo live in the toolbar or its overflow menu.
- **Text and sticky notes:** Pick the text tool and tap the page to type. Pick the sticky-note tool and tap to place one, or drag to set its size.
- **Navigate:** Use the page browser to jump between pages, pinch to zoom, and use the hand tool to pan. Infinite canvases pan and zoom freely in any direction.

See [Writing and editing](writing.md) for shape assist, layers, handwriting recognition, and writing follow, and [Gestures and shortcuts](shortcuts.md) for keyboard and stylus controls.

## Export and back up your work

- **Share a readable copy:** Use the notebook export menu to save a PDF or PNG image.
- **Back up one editable notebook:** Choose **Save as Folio backup** to keep all pages, assets, and undo history in a `.folio` file.
- **Back up your library:** Open **Settings → Backup & restore → Save library backup** (or set up automatic backups to a folder).

These options serve different purposes; read [Data, accounts, and settings](data-and-settings.md) before moving or deleting data.

## Explore other destinations

- **VCAA:** Search and import exam papers directly as PDF notebooks.
- **Mistakes, Study, Progress, and Planner:** Review questions, time study sessions, track exam scores, and plan your week (account-synced features use Focal). See [Study and exams](study.md).
- **Music:** A separate library for sheet-music PDFs with a dedicated reader and metronome. See [Music](music.md).
- **Settings:** Customise appearance, writing feel, stylus and touch, backups, AI review, and accounts. Use the search bar to jump straight to any setting.
